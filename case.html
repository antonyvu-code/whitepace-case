Build decisions

Where I went beyond a straight copy to make the rebuild sharper and more robust than the source.

01

Vector, not raster

The template's UI mockups were flat images. I redrew them as inline SVG — crisp at any resolution, themable, and a fraction of the weight.

02

Interactive pricing

The static monthly/yearly toggle became a real control that animates the price with an eased count-up, driven by data attributes.

03

Accessibility first

Skip link, visible focus rings, real WCAG-contrast pairings, ARIA-labelled illustrations, and a full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.

04

Motion with restraint

GSAP + ScrollTrigger reveal content on entry and power the testimonial carousel — subtle enough to feel premium, never distracting.
